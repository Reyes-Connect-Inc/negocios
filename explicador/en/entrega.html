Explainer for your business

Delivery, step by step

From payment to your files. Timeline: 5 business days from your complete form for the single explainer, 10 for the series of 3, and one a week for the monthly plan. Days that wait on your reply don't count.

1. We get your payment

We contact you the same business day, Monday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., at the phone or email you gave us, and send you the start form. If you pay outside those hours, we contact you the next business day. If the week filled up with a payment that came in at the same time, we tell you the real start date; if it doesn't work for you, we refund everything.

2. You fill out the form (10 minutes)

  1. Your business name as you write it, and the phone or address you want at the end.
  2. The topic in one sentence and who it's for.
  3. 3 to 5 facts that must appear, with where they come from.
  4. What the viewer should do: call, send a keyword or visit.
  5. Logo and colors, or «use the ones on my site».
  6. 0 to 8 photos or clips, if you want.
  7. If your trade requires a license: your license number or your broker's name.
  8. Who approves and where: text message or email.

If something is missing, we ask only for that, in one message.

3. We review the topic the same day

A person checks for promised results, facts without a source and topics we don't do. If the topic can't be done, we tell you and refund everything that same day.

4. We send you the script (1 business day)

In English and Spanish, with a sketch of each scene. It uses only your facts: we don't invent numbers, testimonials or offers. You send your changes in one message or reply «approved». If you'd rather not continue, reply «I won't continue» and we refund everything. Without your «approved» we produce nothing.

5. We produce the video (1 business day)

The vertical video in both languages, with a computer-made voice, captions you can read on a phone and the 4 endings per language. A person reviews it before sending it: every fact matches your form, the captions say the same as the voice and nothing gets cut off.

6. You see the first cut

It carries a «DRAFT» mark. You send your changes in one message (text, order, voice, colors or ending) or reply «done». Changes come back in 1 business day.

7. You get your files

8 MP4 videos without watermark (2 languages times 4 endings), 2 covers, 2 caption files and suggested post text for each network, in a folder with a download link valid for 30 days. We ask whether we can use your video as a sample; you choose «yes, with my name», «yes, without my name» or «no».

The monthly plan, week by week

  • Friday: you send next week's topic, or we pick one from your approved list.
  • Monday: script.
  • Tuesday: your approval.
  • Wednesday: production and review.
  • Thursday: delivery.

If a script isn't approved in time, that video moves to the next week; no more than 2 stack up.

Refund rules are in the terms.
