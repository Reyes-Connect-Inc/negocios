What you get

Every explainer comes with this:

The video

Ready to post

  • A 40 to 60 second vertical video about the topic your business needs to explain.
  • Voice from the first second, large captions and a new image every 2 to 3 seconds.
  • In English and Spanish.
  • An ending for each network: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube Shorts. That's 8 MP4 files, with your phone, your keyword or your address at the end.

Around it

So you can use it

  • A cover for each language.
  • Captions as a separate file too.
  • Suggested post text for each network.
  • Two rounds of changes: one to the script and one to the video.
  • You can use it for your business on your own channels: your social media, your website and your store.
What we don't do
  • Film at your place or use real people's faces.
  • Clone anyone's voice. The voice is computer-made.

  • Promise views, customers or sales.
  • Topics about loans, credit, investments or health.

How it's made

Five steps. Your part is about 10 minutes and two replies.

  1. You choose your option and payBy card, through a Stripe payment link in the name of Reyes Connect Inc.
  2. You fill out a 10-minute formThe topic, 3 to 5 facts with their source, what the viewer should do, your logo and colors, and up to 8 photos or clips if you want. If your trade requires a license, your license number.
  3. You approve the scriptWe send it as text. You ask for changes once or reply «approved». If you'd rather not continue, you get all your money back.
  4. You see the first cutIn English and Spanish. You ask for changes once or reply «done».
  5. You get your filesThe 8 videos, the covers, the captions and the post texts, in one folder to download.

Timeline: 5 business days from when your complete form arrives (10 for the series of 3). Days the script or video wait on your reply don't count. See delivery step by step.
