What you get

Two services. Take one or both.

Service 1

Your website

  • One long page with up to 6 sections:
    1. A cover with your business name and photo.
    2. Your services, with the prices you give us.
    3. A gallery with up to 12 of your photos.
    4. A link to your Google reviews.
    5. Your hours, with address and map.
    6. Buttons to call you and to message you on WhatsApp.
  • In Spanish and in English.
  • Built to look good on a phone.
  • If you already use a booking app, a “Book” button that opens it.
  • One round of changes before it goes live.
  • A web address for your page from day one. If you want your own .com, you buy it in your name, we guide you by phone, and we connect it at no charge.
  • The first month of hosting.
  • From the second month on, the monthly fee covers hosting, the security certificate, and up to 2 minor changes per month (text, photos, hours, or prices).
What is not included
  • The cost of your own domain, if you want one. It is paid separately to the company that registers it.
  • An online store or payments.
  • A new booking system.
  • A new logo.
  • A photo or video shoot.
  • Monthly search engine optimization.
  • Paid ads.
  • Email with your business name.

Service 2

Monthly content for your social media

  • 12 pieces per month, 3 per week: 8 images or carousels and 4 short vertical videos of 15 to 30 seconds, made with the photos and clips you send us.
  • The text for each post, in Spanish or in both languages.
  • The calendar for the month, which you approve before anything goes out.
  • Posting to your Facebook and Instagram with partner access to your page (we never ask for passwords), or the pieces ready for you to post.
  • At the end of the month, a summary of what went out.
What is not included
  • Replying to messages or comments.
  • Paid ads or their budget.
  • Filming at your location.
  • Other networks.
  • Any promised number of followers, reach, or sales.

How it works

Tap each item to see the details.

How you pay
  • By card, through a payment link in the name of Reyes Connect Inc.
  • You pay for the website once you have seen it finished and tell us “publish”. Until then you pay nothing.
  • The content is paid each month in advance.
  • Before the first charge for each service, we send you in writing what you get, how much you will be charged, how often, and how to cancel. We only charge after you reply “I accept”.
  • After that, monthly fees are charged automatically to the same card each month, until you cancel.
  • We do not take cash or payments to personal accounts.
  • There is no long-term contract.
When it is delivered
  • The website: ready in 3 to 5 days, counted from when you send us your photos and details. That day we show it to you finished.
  • The content: first you receive the calendar for the month to approve; then the pieces go out 3 per week.
Before it goes live
  • We show you your finished website, with your photos, as an image. You ask for the changes you want in one round.
  • When you tell us “publish”, you pay and we put it online. Your website does not go on the internet before that.
  • If you see it and decide not to publish it, you pay nothing and we delete it.
  • With the content, nothing goes out until you approve the calendar.
  • Once the website is live, you can ask for up to 2 minor changes each month.
Refunds
  • The website: there is nothing to refund, because you pay after seeing it finished.
  • The content: if you do not approve the first month’s calendar before the first piece goes out, we refund that first month in full. After that, there are no refunds.
How to cancel
  • You can cancel anytime before the next charge: tell us by message or by phone at (786) 835-2214, or by email at hello@reyesconnectapp.com, and you are not charged again.
  • If you cancel hosting, the page goes offline at the end of the paid month. If you bought a domain, it is yours and stays with you.
  • If you cancel the content and have a website, the website continues with its monthly hosting fee.
